An accountancy approach that is right for you

Ruth Elliott is the principal of the practice, and an experienced Chartered Accountant.

The practice started in 2013 to provide a range of business and tax services to local clients in Northwich. It offers business and tax services to businesses, individuals and small companies.

We come alongside clients to provide support, help them manage their businesses efficiently and enable them to grow. The business is focused on building strong client relationships; we think this is key to our success. Our services are tailored to the needs of each client and we always strive to plan for their future financial growth.

Taking on new clients

We are currently close to capacity and generally unable to take on any new clients at this time. We will consider taking on new clients if they are referred to us by existing clients. We will update this notice if the situation changes.

How we work

Personal

We usually meet all our clients in person at the start of the engagement. We get to know you and learn about you and your business and what makes it work. Every business is unique.

Tailored to your needs

We work closely with you and your business to get to know you and the challenges you face. Clients are encouraged to ask for advice when they need it. We aim to respond to all queries promptly.

Professional

We set high standards for the quality of our work and keep up to date with the latest developments in tax and accounts compliance requirements. We undertake training to further our knowledge. We aim to provide each client with the best service possible.

What we do

Company services

We enable clients to run their companies efficiently.

  • We can set up your company for you, and keep you compliant with Companies House.
  • We will help you manage your tax obligations including payroll taxes, corporation tax and VAT.
  • We will help you as company owners with managing your company finances and provide tax efficient solutions for taking money from the business.
  • We can help you to gain insights into your business's financial health. We can analyse profitability, help you manage cashflow, and make informed decisions to drive growth and success.

Business accounts

We help clients manage their businesses and their accounts.

  • We can advise you on the best structure for your new business, or whether a change in structure is appropriate.
  • We provide you with QuickBooks software for keeping your accounts, or work with you using your preferred bookkeeping solution.
  • We will provide you with ongoing support for your bookkeeping and finalise your accounts following the year-end.
  • We will prepare your financial statements, which help you comply with statutory, tax or other stakeholder responsibilities.

Tax services

We advise our clients on their tax issues and complete tax returns for clients.

  • We can provide you with advice on your tax problem, and help you find tax efficient solutions.
  • We will support you with information to help you stay compliant with the latest tax laws and regulations.
  • We will support your business with complying with payroll tax, VAT and other tax compliance requirements.
  • We will complete your personal tax return and any additional relevant sections, such as for property income or capital gains.
  • We will advise you of the tax due, due dates, and other compliance requirements.

Our fees

Value

Our fees are structured to reflect the value we bring to the business. Our services are cost effective and support clients to meet their goals.

Transparent

We will usually quote for the fees before starting any work, so you know what to expect. If we encounter any issues that may impact on our fee, we will raise this with you before proceeding further.

Fee structure

We discuss your needs with you and tailor the services to match your requirements. Each client is unique and our service packages reflect that. Fees depend on the complexity of the work required, the volume of transactions and so on.

Ongoing support

We will provide services on a recurring basis for as long as you require our support. This may be annually for tax returns and accounts, or monthly (for example bookkeeping support and payroll), depending on client requirements.

Becoming a client

We take pride in building trusted relationships with our clients. Most of our clients come through referrals, which gives a strong foundation of mutual trust. Here is how we welcome new clients.

  1. Referral and initial contact

    Our practice usually operates at close to capacity, so we normally only accept new clients through referral. But occasionally we will take on new clients if they contact us directly.

    Once you have contacted us we'll confirm if we have availability and then reach out to schedule a convenient time for an initial phone consultation.

  2. Phone consultation

    During the phone consultation, we'll discuss your specific accounting needs, goals and challenges. We'll provide an overview of our services and discuss likely fees based on your requirements.

    This is an opportunity for you to ask questions and ensure we're the right fit for your financial needs.

  3. Client meeting

    If you choose to proceed, we'll arrange a meeting to verify your identification and comply with regulatory requirements. This meeting is usually in person, but we can arrange a virtual meeting if required. It also gives you the opportunity to ask any additional questions and to discuss your needs in greater detail.

    There is usually no fee charged for the meeting unless you require specific advice at this stage, for example on capital gains tax or what business structure to use. If a fee will be chargeable we will inform you, and the fee is payable in advance.

  4. Service clarification and engagement

    We'll clarify the scope of services tailored to your needs and provide a clear outline of what to expect. We will send you a fee quote or provide an estimate. We normally confirm the actual fee once we see the paperwork or business records.

    If you currently have an accountant we will ask for their details and contact them to begin the handover process.

    We will send you a formal engagement letter detailing the agreed services. This forms the basis of your contract with us. Once you review and sign the engagement letter, we'll begin delivering our accounting services.
